Idea Atlas

Clickable Political Vocabulary

Every important word needs a plain definition, context, common confusion, related figures, and a test path.

Class Struggle

Conflict between social classes over labor, property, power, and who receives the value created in society.

Social Democracy

A reform path that uses democratic institutions, welfare systems, unions, and public goods within mixed economies.

Market

A system of exchange using prices, supply, demand, and decentralized decisions. Powerful, but not magic or morally complete.

Authoritarianism

Power concentrated without meaningful accountability, dissent, or free opposition. Can appear under different flags.

PSDS Judgment Chamber

Truth Checks

These cards keep promise, power, failure, warning, and open questions visible at the same time.

Capitalism

Promise
Freedom, markets, innovation, prosperity.
Failure
Inequality, exploitation, monopoly, instability.
Warning
Wealth can dominate politics.
Question
How do we preserve freedom without letting money rule everything?

Socialism

Promise
Fairness, dignity, public goods, worker power.
Failure
Bureaucracy, coercion, weak incentives in some systems.
Warning
Equality can become coercion if power centralizes.
Question
How do we build solidarity without crushing freedom?

Communism

Promise
Classless society, common ownership, end of exploitation.
Failure
Many party-state experiments became authoritarian.
Warning
Revolutionary states can create new ruling classes.
Question
Can common ownership exist with real democracy and dissent?

Markets

Promise
Decentralized exchange and price signals.
Failure
Inequality, externalities, monopoly, commodification.
Warning
Not everything valuable should be priced.
Question
Where do markets belong, and where do they not belong?
