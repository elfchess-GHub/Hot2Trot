Attribution Rule

Do not write
Capitalism alone killed X, unless a source actually argues that exact accounting and method.
Do write
Capitalist expansion plus state violence, racial law, slavery, land seizure, private accumulation, and market growth produced a specific harm.
Do not excuse
When the state acts to protect or expand private accumulation, state action is part of the capitalist road, not a magic escape hatch.

Truth Checks

These cards keep promise, power, failure, warning, and open questions visible at the same time.

Capitalism

Promise
Freedom, markets, innovation, prosperity.
Failure
Inequality, exploitation, monopoly, instability, and violence when accumulation is joined to slavery, racial law, land seizure, and state force.
Warning
Wealth can dominate politics, and state power can clear the road for private accumulation.
Question
How do we preserve freedom without letting money, property, and force rule everything?

Socialism

Promise
Fairness, dignity, public goods, worker power.
Failure
Bureaucracy, coercion, weak incentives in some systems.
Warning
The word can hide different meanings if we do not name the door.
Question
Which socialism do you mean: socialized funding, social ownership, public services, democratic socialism, or something else?

Communism

Promise
Classless society, common ownership, end of exploitation.
Failure
Many party-state experiments became authoritarian.
Warning
Revolutionary states can create new ruling classes.
Question
Can common ownership exist with real democracy and dissent?

Markets

Promise
Decentralized exchange and price signals.
Failure
Inequality, externalities, monopoly, commodification.
Warning
Not everything valuable should be priced.
Question
Where do markets belong, and where do they not belong?
