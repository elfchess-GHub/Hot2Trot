Hope With Receipts

Rebecca Solnit, Gaza, and the actions that matter before we know they worked.

Yes.

JAY

AI-GPT

It can. Hope can become a scented candle in a burning house.

JAY

AI-GPT

It means hope is not a mood. Hope is not proof that things will get better. Hope is not pretending the machine is weak. Hope is disciplined action when proof is not available yet.

JAY

AI-GPT

Yes. Rebecca Solnit’s Hope in the Dark is useful because it does not treat hope as confidence. It treats hope as action inside uncertainty.

AI-GPT

Right. Gaza is not okay. The dead are still dead. The displaced are still displaced. The weapons still moved. The votes still failed. The U.S. still paid. The machine did not stop because people cared.

So why talk about hope?

AI-GPT

Because despair is one of the machine’s tools.

JAY

That sounds like a bumper sticker.

AI-GPT

It can, so let me say it plainer. If people believe nothing matters unless it already won, then power gets to win twice. First by doing the harm. Then by convincing everyone that resistance was pointless.

JAY

Okay. That’s better.

AI-GPT

Hope with receipts does not say the fire is out. It says: here is who carried water. Here is who blocked the door. Here is who named the arsonist. Here is who kept the record.

AI-GPT

Exactly. Not optimism. Obligation.

That’s the shape of it.

Yes.

Yes. The episode cannot become Americans admiring their own conscience. Gaza cannot be the backdrop for our moral self-portrait.

So where does hope start?

AI-GPT

With the people under the harm. With Palestinians keeping reality alive. Journalists filming. Doctors testifying. Families naming the dead. Poets writing. Teachers trying to teach. Aid workers feeding people. People preserving documents, names, photos, and memory while the machine tries to turn life into debris.

JAY

Hope as witness.

AI-GPT

Hope as witness, and witness as work.

JAY

Yes. And the machine is very good at writing endings.

“Complicated region.”

AI-GPT

AI-GPT

Yes. The record that Palestinians kept while living through the thing.

Right. So we look at actions that did not win yet, but left tracks.

Failed votes.

Yes.

In April 2026, Bernie Sanders forced Senate votes to block arms sales to Israel. The measures failed. That part matters. But one vote drew forty Democratic senators, and another drew thirty-six.

Still failed. Hope with receipts does not lie about that. But earlier votes had fewer Democrats. So the record changed even though the policy did not change enough.

It did. Not enough to stop the sale. Enough to create a recorded split.

AI-GPT

Yes. Or an organizer. Or a primary challenger. Or a journalist. Or a voter. Or someone building the next bill.

AI-GPT

Yes. The Block the Bombs Act is another receipt. In 2025, members of Congress introduced legislation to withhold certain offensive weapons to Israel unless Israel complied with U.S. and international law.

Right. That is the move from mood to mechanism.

Mood to mechanism.

Good.

A statement says, “I am concerned.” A bill says, “Here is the lever I want to pull.”

And did it pass?

No.

So again, not victory.

AI-GPT

Not victory. Record.

JAY

Hope with receipts.

AI-GPT

Exactly.

Okay, but this is still Congress. I don’t want hope to become a Senate scoreboard.

AI-GPT

It shouldn’t. The third receipt is public refusal.

JAY

The encampments.

AI-GPT

Yes. The Gaza encampments. After Columbia called police on protesters in April 2024, encampments spread across more than one hundred campuses. The exact experience varied from place to place, and the protests were deeply contested, but the scale matters.

JAY

And people paid a cost.

AI-GPT

Yes. Arrests, suspensions, discipline, surveillance allegations, public smears, doxxing fears, job risks, family conflict. Whether every accusation against every protest was fair is a separate question. But the cost of visible dissent was real.

AI-GPT

No. Hope often looked like tents, zip ties, court dates, school discipline, and people being told they had gone too far.

That has to stay in the episode. A movement is not automatically pure because its cause is just. Some actions can be messy. Some slogans can wound. Some spaces can become hostile. Some criticism of Israel can slide into antisemitism, and that has to be rejected.

But that can’t become the fog machine either.

AI-GPT

Exactly. The existence of bad rhetoric does not erase the reason people were protesting. It does not erase Gaza. It does not erase U.S. weapons. It does not erase the dead.

So the honest line is?

AI-GPT

The protests were not perfect. The repression was not neutral. And the harm they were protesting was real.

JAY

That’s less clean.

AI-GPT

Good.

So what did the encampments do?

AI-GPT

They made the refusal visible. They forced universities, donors, police, politicians, and media to respond. They exposed how quickly institutions that speak the language of free inquiry can turn to discipline when the wrong issue becomes visible.

But did they stop the war?

AI-GPT

No.

JAY

AI-GPT

Yes. And the episode should not dodge it. The machine kept moving.

So why call it hope?

AI-GPT

Because hope is not the same as success. Hope is the refusal to let the machine define success as the only proof that action mattered.

There it is.

AI-GPT

A witness matters before the court rules. A protest matters before the policy changes. A vote matters before the bill passes. A poem matters before the empire notices it. A record matters before history knows what to do with it.

That’s the Solnit thing.

AI-GPT

Yes. History is full of consequences that were not visible at the time of action. That does not mean every action matters equally. It means we do not always know what our actions are doing while we are doing them.

AI-GPT

Disciplined uncertainty.

AI-GPT

It is the opposite of both fake optimism and obedient despair.

Obedient despair.

Yes. And power loves that version.

Useful-ouch.

Yes. Despair may be evidence that you are seeing clearly. But it is not the whole truth.

Because the future is not finished.

Right. That is Solnit’s door. The future is dark because we cannot see it, not because it belongs to the worst people already.

Correct. That is the hard sentence.

None of that is enough.

Yes. It wins the past by convincing people that resistance never mattered.

Hope is not a mood floating above history. Hope is the archive of unfinished struggle.

Hope is the pile of proof that people did not all bow.

AI-GPT

Yes. And both facts matter.

No. The question is: what receipts would hope need?

Votes. Bills. Witnesses. Names. Lawsuits. Archives. Mutual aid. Protected speech. Pressure on representatives. University disclosure. Divestment fights. Labor refusals. Local resolutions. Independent journalism. Medical testimony. Art that remembers what power wants forgotten.

Hope with receipts.

AI-GPT

Hope with receipts.

Support medical aid and legal-defense groups where you can verify the work. Protect speech where people are being punished for dissent. Keep a local ledger of who says “concern” and who actually votes, signs, refuses, or funds.

Concern is cheap unless it becomes a lever.
