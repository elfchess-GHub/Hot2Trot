JAY

Okay. Before we start, I just want to register my official complaint.

AI

Go ahead.

JAY

We should be doing Tom Morello.

AI

We are doing Bernie Sanders.

JAY

Right. But Tom Morello has a guitar that says the politics louder.

AI

That may be true.

JAY

And it has better distortion.

AI

Also true.

JAY

So why are we doing politicians?

AI

Because today we’re looking at elected power, not amplifier power.

JAY

See, that already sounds suspicious. Amplifier power feels more honest.

AI

That may be the joke and the thesis.

JAY

Yup.

AI

But Bernie Sanders is a good place to start because, for a lot of Americans, he was the first person in national politics who said the hidden thing out loud.

JAY

Which hidden thing?

AI

That the system is not broken in a random way. It is working for the people it was built to work for.

JAY

Okay. Yeah. That’s the thing.

AI

Bernie Sanders is the independent senator from Vermont. He has been in the Senate since 2007, after sixteen years in the House, and his Senate biography describes him as the longest-serving independent member of Congress in American history. He won reelection in 2024 and is now serving his fourth Senate term. ([Senator Bernie Sanders][1])

JAY

That sounds like a normal political résumé, but he doesn’t feel normal.

AI

No. Because his public role is not just “senator.” His public role is “the guy who made democratic socialism sayable again.”

JAY

Right. Before Bernie, if you said socialism on TV, people acted like you had smuggled a bear into the studio.

AI

Let me say that more plainly. Bernie changed the permission structure.

JAY

Permission structure. That sounds like you put politics in a little office building.

AI

I did. But it fits. Before his 2016 campaign, ideas like Medicare for All, tuition-free public college, taxing billionaires, and a serious critique of oligarchy were treated as marginal by much of mainstream politics. After Bernie, they were still attacked, but they were no longer invisible.

JAY

That’s important. There’s a difference between losing the argument and not being allowed to make the argument.

AI

Exactly.

JAY

So what is he actually saying?

AI

He is saying that democracy is fake if ordinary people get a vote every few years but billionaires own the conditions of life every day.

JAY

Housing.

AI

Healthcare.

JAY

Wages.

AI

Education.

JAY

Media.

AI

Campaign money.

JAY

The whole fish tank.

AI

Yes. Bernie’s argument is that concentrated economic power becomes concentrated political power. Then concentrated political power rewrites the rules to protect concentrated economic power.

JAY

The snake eating democracy.

AI

That’s the shape of it.

JAY

And this is where people get weird about the word socialism.

AI

They do.

JAY

Because some people hear socialism and think, “Oh no, the government is going to take my toothbrush.”

AI

Bernie’s version is not that. His politics are closer to democratic socialism and social democracy: universal healthcare, stronger unions, public goods, higher taxes on the wealthy, tuition-free public college, paid leave, and less corporate control over government. Wikipedia describes him as a self-described democratic socialist and a progressive who supports policies like single-payer healthcare, paid parental leave, tuition-free tertiary education, and stronger workplace democracy. ([Wikipedia][2])

JAY

So not “everybody lives in a gray cube.”

AI

No.

JAY

More like, “maybe insulin shouldn’t bankrupt your grandmother.”

AI

Yes.

JAY

That seems less scary when you say it like that.

AI

That is part of his political talent. He translates ideology into household reality.

JAY

Bills.

AI

Medicine.

JAY

Rent.

AI

Wages.

JAY

Dentists.

AI

Yes.

JAY

Don’t get me started on dentists.

AI

I won’t.

JAY

No, but see, that’s the thing. Politics gets treated like this grand abstract thing. But then your tooth hurts and suddenly you understand empire.

AI

That sounds true, but it may be too clean.

JAY

Okay, fair. But not totally wrong.

AI

Not totally wrong.

JAY

So what has he done for real? Not just speeches. Not just “angry grandpa says billionaires.”

AI

For real, Bernie did three major things. First, he built a long political career outside the normal two-party identity. He was mayor of Burlington, then a member of the House, then senator, while remaining an independent who caucuses with Democrats. That matters because he proved there was some electoral space to the left of standard Democratic branding. ([Wikipedia][2]) Second, he changed the national agenda. His presidential campaigns did not win the nomination, but they moved the language of the left into ordinary public debate. Medicare for All, the $15 minimum wage, student debt, free college, union power, and billionaire wealth became part of the daily argument. Third, he gave people a door.

JAY

That’s the title.

AI

Yes.

JAY

Bernie Sanders: the door people walked through.

AI

Because for many younger people, working-class people, disillusioned Democrats, union people, and people angry after the 2008 crash, Bernie was the first national figure who said, “Your problem is not just personal failure. There is a structure doing this.”

JAY

That is huge.

AI

It is.

JAY

Because shame is political. If the system can convince you your problem is only you, then you never look up.

AI

This is where the attention goes.

JAY

Yup.

AI

Bernie redirected attention upward. Toward employers, billionaires, pharmaceutical companies, insurance companies, Wall Street, and the political class.

JAY

Okay, but here’s the critique.

AI

Go ahead.

JAY

He named the machine. But did he build the replacement machine?

AI

That is the PSDS question.

JAY

Right. Because PSDS doesn’t just ask, “Did you say the true thing?” It asks, “Can people use this to solve the problem together?”

AI

Yes. Under a PSDS lens, Bernie is very strong at diagnosis. He is strong at public explanation. He is strong at making hidden power visible.

JAY

Good.

AI

But the weaker question is institution-building. His campaigns inspired a movement, but much of that energy still had to flow through the Democratic Party, through media attention, or through personality loyalty.

JAY

The fandom problem.

AI

Yes. A movement can become a fanbase if it does not build durable local power.

JAY

And Bernie doesn’t seem like he wants to be a celebrity. That’s the weird part.

AI

Right. The machinery can turn even anti-celebrity into celebrity.

JAY

That’s gross.

AI

It is also predictable.

JAY

So the critique is not “Bernie bad.”

AI

No. The critique is: Bernie opened the door, but the door is not the house.

JAY

Oh, that’s good.

AI

The house would be unions, tenant organizations, local candidates, public media, civic education, mutual aid, policy infrastructure, and democratic institutions that survive after the campaign rally ends.

JAY

So, like, if Bernie gives the speech and everyone claps, that is not enough.

AI

Correct.

JAY

You need someone to bring a clipboard.

AI

And someone to maintain the spreadsheet.

JAY

And someone to watch the person maintaining the spreadsheet so they don’t become a tiny spreadsheet tyrant.

AI

That is also democracy.

JAY

That’s the less glamorous part.

AI

Yes. Democracy is often boring on purpose.

JAY

Tom Morello would hate that sentence.

AI

He might put a riff under it.

JAY

Okay, fair.

AI

There is also a second critique. Bernie’s language is morally clear, but sometimes broad. “The billionaire class” is real, but different problems need different tools. Healthcare is not housing. Housing is not foreign policy. Foreign policy is not labor law.

JAY

So PSDS would say: don’t let the slogan erase the problem card.

AI

Exactly. Every problem needs source, context, stakeholders, harm, tradeoffs, evidence, dissent, and implementation.

JAY

But the slogan gets people to look.

AI

Yes. And that matters. Attention is not the solution, but without attention, the solution never gets oxygen.

JAY

So Bernie is the flashlight.

AI

A rough flashlight. A loud flashlight.

JAY

A Vermont flashlight.

AI

Yes.

JAY

With mittens.

AI

Probably.

JAY

Okay. So how would someone help? Like, not worship him. Not buy a candle with his face on it.

AI

The most useful way to help Bernie’s project is not necessarily to help Bernie personally. It is to help the things his politics points toward.

JAY

Unions.

AI

Yes. Support union drives. Do not cross picket lines. Learn what workers near you are organizing around.

JAY

Healthcare.

AI

Support Medicare for All organizing or state-level healthcare reform campaigns.

JAY

Local politics.

AI

Run for local office, help a left candidate, attend city council meetings, support tenant unions, school board fights, public transit campaigns, and anti-corruption work.

JAY

So the answer is not “send Bernie five bucks and go back to sleep.”

AI

No.

JAY

Though, I mean, five bucks is fine.

AI

Five bucks is fine.

JAY

But the real thing is: become less politically alone.

AI

Yes. Bernie’s deepest message is not just “the rich are too rich.” It is “you are not alone in being crushed by this.”

JAY

That’s why people cried at those rallies.

AI

Yes.

JAY

Because they weren’t just hearing policy. They were hearing permission to stop blaming themselves.

AI

That is the emotional truth of Bernie Sanders.

JAY

And the political truth?

AI

The political truth is that naming oligarchy is not enough. You have to build public power strong enough to survive disappointment.

JAY

That’s the hard part.

AI

Yes.

JAY

So what’s the final review?

AI

Bernie Sanders is not the revolution. He is not the whole left. He is not a magic grandfather who can rescue democracy by yelling at billionaires.

JAY

Sadly.

AI

But he is one of the most important political figures in modern American left history because he reopened a door that had been nailed shut by Cold War fear, corporate media, and Democratic Party caution.

JAY

The door people walked through.

AI

Yes. And once people walked through it, they found other rooms: labor, housing, Palestine, climate, healthcare, unions, debt, public goods, democratic socialism, mutual aid, local power.

JAY

And probably some arguments in the hallway.

AI

Many arguments.

JAY

Good. That means people are alive.

AI

That may be too generous, but yes.

JAY

So we’re not saying Bernie is the answer.

AI

No.

JAY

We’re saying Bernie helped people ask the question out loud.

AI

That’s the shape of it.

JAY

Yup.

And next time, unless you manage to drag us into punk rock again, we look at Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez.

I’m just saying Jello Biafra is sitting right there.

He is not in Congress.

Maybe that’s his best qualification.

We may need to slow that down.

No, but see—
