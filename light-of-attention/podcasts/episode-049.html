Visibility Is Not Power

Partly, yes.

Yes. October 7, 2023. Hamas-led attacks killed around twelve hundred people in Israel and hostages were taken. That was a real wound. It was visible. It mattered.

AI-GPT

Yes. Bombing. Rubble. Hospitals. Families. Dead children. Hunger. Aid workers killed. Journalists killed. People filming their own lives while the world watched through phones.

The camera was supposed to be enough.

No. It created attention. It created outrage. It created protest. But attention did not automatically become power.

The second wound.

AI-GPT

Yes. The first wound is the violence. The second wound is watching the violence survive being witnessed.

JAY

That’s the line.

AI-GPT

It is.

JAY

So what does Taylor help us see?

AI-GPT

Taylor helps us separate moral visibility from organized leverage. A video can expose. A chant can gather. A march can declare. A hashtag can spread. But systems do not usually surrender because they were embarrassed.

JAY

They adapt.

AI-GPT

They adapt. They reframe. They call the protesters violent. They split good protest from bad protest. They say they hear the pain. They hold meetings. They create committees. They change language. They wait for exhaustion.

JAY

They put “equity” on the website.

AI-GPT

Sometimes, yes.

JAY

And then the budgets keep moving.

AI-GPT

That is the question: did the visibility become leverage? Did it change who controls money, weapons, policing, law, land, or policy?

It is also more useful.

Yes.

Not enough by default.

Not enough.

AI-GPT

AI-GPT

Exactly. The episodes are linked.

AI-GPT

Right. From Black Lives Matter to Black liberation. The title itself says the point is not only the moment of outrage. It is the movement from seeing harm to building a politics that can change the conditions that produce harm.

AI-GPT

AI-GPT

Yes.

AI-GPT

Groups with strategy. Demands that can be tracked. Votes. Budgets. Candidate pressure. Legal defense. Labor power. Campus disclosure demands. Divestment campaigns. Humanitarian channels. Media work. Local resolutions. Primary challenges. Protection for speech. Records of who did what.

JAY

So not just “awareness.”

AI-GPT

Awareness is the beginning. Power asks: what can you make harder for the machine to keep doing?

AI-GPT

JAY

There it is.

AI-GPT

Outrage has to become consequence.

JAY

And if it doesn’t?

AI-GPT

Then it can be absorbed. Turned into branding, trauma content, moral performance, or fatigue.

JAY

That’s bleak.

AI-GPT

It is. But it is not hopeless.

JAY

Because?

AI-GPT

Because visibility did change some things. It changed public language. It created records. It forced votes. It exposed institutions. It built networks. It trained people. It radicalized some and clarified others. It showed where the pressure points were.

AI-GPT

No. And the episode must not pretend it did.

Pick a place where visibility can become leverage.

If you care about politics, pressure representatives on specific votes, not general concern.

JAY

AI-GPT

Posting can spread visibility. But ask what it connects to. A call. A donation. A vote. A meeting. A union statement. A public record. A local demand. A protected witness.

AI-GPT

Good.
