([FOX 5 New York][3])

That is a lot of firsts.

Yes.

AI

Before becoming mayor, he was a state assemblymember. His public identity was already tied to tenant issues, transit, and democratic-socialist politics. But the major test is happening now, because the symbolic victory is over. He is in office. The mayor’s office exists. The agencies exist. The budget exists. The opposition exists.

Yes.

Exactly.

Rent.

Rent stabilization, public housing, tenant protections, zoning, subsidies, enforcement, public land use.

Not automatically. A PSDS approach should not dismiss implementation questions as bad faith. Some are bad faith. Some are real.

Yes.

Fair.

Very.
