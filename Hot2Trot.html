Foundations of Marxism (19th Century)

Karl Marx (1818–1883)

Role: Theorist of capitalism & socialism
Memory Hook: Marx = class struggle & exploitation
  • Class struggle
  • Surplus value
  • Means of production

Marx analyzed capitalism as a system of exploitation where workers create more value than they receive. He argued that history is shaped by class struggle and that economic structures shape politics, law, and culture.

Friedrich Engels (1820–1895)

Role: Marx’s collaborator
Memory Hook: Engels = industry + Marxism
  • Working-class conditions
  • Communist Manifesto

Engels brought real industrial experience to Marxist theory and helped support and publish Marx’s work. He grounded Marxism in factory conditions and labor realities.

Early Socialist Debates

Eduard Bernstein (1850–1932)

Role: Revisionist socialist
Memory Hook: Bernstein = reform, not revolution
  • Revisionism
  • Social democracy

Bernstein argued that socialism should be achieved through gradual democratic reforms rather than violent revolution.

Karl Kautsky (1854–1938)

Role: Orthodox Marxist theorist
Memory Hook: Kautsky = Marx without Lenin
  • Orthodox Marxism
  • Second International

Kautsky supported Marxist theory but opposed Lenin’s authoritarian methods. He believed socialism should come through democratic mass parties.

Eugene V. Debs (1855–1926)

Role: U.S. labor & socialist leader
Memory Hook: Debs = American socialism
  • Socialist Party of America
