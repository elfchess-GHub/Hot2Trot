What DubiouSDungeons Is

DubiouSDungeons is a cooperative and competitive dungeon board-game prototype. It supports 1-4 local pass-and-play players, lets each player choose a class and portrait, and sends the group through a three-level dungeon of rooms, roles, puzzles, and resource checks.

Pass-And-Play

Start a local table for one to four players, with human and bot seats supported while the table moves through the dungeon.

Hidden Roles

Players carry a secret role letter in the bag, adding a layer of uncertainty to the dungeon run.

Room Encounters

Monster, Trap, Jail, Market, Temple, and stair rooms rotate through each level in a random order.
