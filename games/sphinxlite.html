What SphinxLite Is

SphinxLite is a lighter rebuild of Sphinx, built to keep the art, characters, and core card-play feeling while making debate easier to read. Instead of simple damage racing, the game treats an argument as a run of cards: what you open with, what you chain next, what stays on the board, and what changes the direction of the debate.

Four Schools

Logic, Empathy, Rhetoric, and Meta shape how cards pressure resolve, composure, clarity, and momentum.

Chained Turns

You begin a line with an Opener, then keep playing as long as the next card legally chains from the current run.

Visible Debate

Lasting cards remain on the run board so the debate has a visible memory instead of disappearing into hidden math.

Debate As Card Play

Build a deck, review your reserve, and carry the argument from camp into the next debate.
