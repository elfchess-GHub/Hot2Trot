Shape-Azoid

A 1-6 player shape-control card game with pass-and-play seats, AI players, Zoids, Penta-Zoids, and colorful space-board chaos.

Latest download View gameplay

Shape Control In Space

Choose 1 to 6 players, set human or AI seats, and play cards that place colored hexes, squares, triangles, Zoids, Penta-Zoids, lines, and wild cards onto a growing board.

The board starts with the first shape. Later shapes attach to exposed sides. Line cards open walls so Zoids can travel through the shape system and change control as they move.

Download portable build

How It Plays

Shape-Azoid is easy to read at a glance: build the board, open routes, let the Zoids move, and score the colors you control.

Cards Build The Board

Hex, square, and triangle cards place those shapes in the card color. Wild cards can be right-clicked to cycle their card type for the round.

Zoids Change Control

Zoids bounce inside the shape system. Wherever a Zoid travels, it can change shape control to its own color.

Penta-Zoids Eat Zoids

Penta-Zoids are larger star balls. They score more, eat normal Zoids they touch, and disappear if two Penta-Zoids collide.

Round Deck Variation

At the start of each round, three card types are drawn like a slot machine to add extra sets or make one card type wild.

Color Scoring

Score played shapes, controlled shapes, Zoids, Penta-Zoids, and lit same-color connections.
