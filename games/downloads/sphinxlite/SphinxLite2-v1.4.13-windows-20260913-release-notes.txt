Changes:
- Fixed the ordinary Thebes Embalmer's Helper encounter so its dedicated
  villager artwork is used instead of the generic fallback portrait.
- Encounter-art lookup now normalizes punctuation and spacing consistently on
  both the requested name and real art-folder name.
- Added the complete Naukris and Harran city-request flow to the normal game.
- New characters discover a request after the first ordinary villager victory
  and reward at city levels 1 through 3.
- Requests support an additional hearing, the described personal help, or
  leaving for later while keeping progress.
- Completed requests award Town Standing once and connect to the existing
  guardian and boss progression requirements.
- The full game, ordinary launcher, normal saving, artwork, music, and effects
  are included. This is not a separate tester edition.
