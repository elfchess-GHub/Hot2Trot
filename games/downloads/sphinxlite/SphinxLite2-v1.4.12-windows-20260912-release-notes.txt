Sphinx: A Philosophy Card Game - v1.4.12 full Windows build

Files:
- SphinxLite2-Windows-Setup-1.4.12.exe
- SphinxLite2-v1.4.12-windows-20260912.zip
- latest.zip is matched to the versioned portable ZIP.
- latest-update.json identifies and verifies the installer used by the updater.

Changes:
- Added the complete Naukris and Harran city-request flow to the normal game.
- New characters discover a request after the first ordinary villager victory
  and reward at city levels 1 through 3.
- Requests support an additional hearing, the described personal help, or
  leaving for later while keeping progress.
- Completed requests award Town Standing once and connect to the existing
  guardian and boss progression requirements.
- The full game, ordinary launcher, normal saving, artwork, music, and effects
  are included. This is not a separate tester edition.

Testing channel:
- New testers can download and extract latest.zip.
- Existing installed testers should start Play SphinxLite2.exe normally; it
  checks the GitHub update manifest and installs the newer complete version.
- itch.io remains on the current public release until a final build is approved.
