Consent

Plain definition: Consent means political authority is legitimate because people agree to it, not because rulers automatically own obedience.

This is the anti-divine-right door into the modern political road.

Why This Idea Matters

Locke ties government to consent so power has a test: it must protect the people and their rights. If government violates that trust, it loses legitimacy. That idea matters later because democratic movements, liberal reformers, and socialist organizers all argue over who really counts in that consent.
