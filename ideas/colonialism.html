Colonialism

Plain definition: Colonialism is rule or domination over another people or territory through empire, settlement, extraction, trade control, law, religion, military force, or chartered companies.

Colonialism is older and broader than capitalism, but the two often became entangled through land, labor, commodities, finance, and global trade.

Why It Matters Here

This keeps the page from pretending all violence began with the United States, while also refusing to use older empires as an excuse for American capitalism. Empire, monarchy, church, chartered companies, slavery, settlement, and market expansion can overlap without becoming one single thing.

Careful Version

Good claim: colonial rule often organized land, labor, resources, and trade for the benefit of the colonizing power.
Bad shortcut: every colonial system was identical, or every colonial harm should be counted as capitalism without explaining the mechanism.
Study question: what is doing the work here: empire, church, monarchy, company power, market demand, settlement, slavery, or state violence?
