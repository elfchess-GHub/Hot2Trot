Interventionism

Plain definition: Interventionism is government action that changes market outcomes through controls, subsidies, mandates, protections, taxes, or regulations.

In the Mises path, interventionism is treated as a distortion of market coordination.

Why It Matters Here

Interventionism matters because capitalist thinkers disagree sharply over when public rules correct markets and when they break them.
