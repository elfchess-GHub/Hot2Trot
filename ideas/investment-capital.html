Investment Capital

Plain definition: Investment capital is money used to buy ownership claims, finance companies, hold assets, earn returns, and compound wealth over time.

In the Buffett path, investment capital is patient ownership rather than direct factory command or platform control.

Why It Matters Here

Investment capital matters because ownership itself can become a governing force in the economy. A person does not have to run a factory floor or hold office to influence corporate priorities; capital allocation can steer what gets built, bought, preserved, or shut down.
