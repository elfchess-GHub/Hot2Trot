Ludwig von Mises

1881-1973 | Topic: Austrian Economics / Socialist Calculation Critique | Era: Austrian School, calculation debate, and anti-interventionism (1920-1973)

Role: Austrian School economist known for the economic calculation problem, defense of markets, criticism of socialism, and influence on libertarian and free-market thought.

Loading Wiki image

Why Mises Is Here

Mises belongs here because capitalism's intellectual defense includes a hard claim about knowledge, prices, private property, and economic calculation.

He matters because his critique of socialism is not only moral; it argues that complex economies need market prices to compare alternatives.

One Clean Idea

Mises argues that without private property in the means of production and market prices, planners cannot rationally calculate complex economic choices.
