Why Buffett Is Here

Buffett belongs here because American capitalism is not only factories, oil, banks, and tech. It is also ownership: buying pieces of companies and letting compounding do political and economic work over time.

He matters because he makes capitalism look calmer than the robber-baron or platform-power versions, while still concentrating enormous wealth and corporate influence.

One Clean Idea

Investment capitalism can look patient and disciplined, but it still raises questions about ownership, compounding, corporate control, taxes, and philanthropy. Buffett is useful because he makes capitalism look less like conquest and more like allocation: who owns, who waits, who compounds, and who gets a say.
