Why Hayek Is Here

Hayek belongs here because the project needs the strongest market-side argument, not a straw man.

He matters because debates over socialism, planning, and markets often turn on how much knowledge any central authority can actually collect and use.

One Clean Idea

Markets can coordinate dispersed information through prices, while centralized planning faces a knowledge problem.
