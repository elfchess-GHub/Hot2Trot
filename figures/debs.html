Eugene V. Debs

1855-1926 | Topic: American Socialism / Labor | Era: Labor politics and democratic speech (1890-1920)

Role: American labor leader, socialist candidate, organizer, speaker, and prisoner for anti-war speech.

Loading Wiki image

Why Debs Is Here

Debs belongs here because he brings the road into American democratic life: railroads, unions, strikes, elections, courts, prisons, and public speech.

He matters because socialism in the United States is not only a foreign scare-word. It also has a labor, free-speech, voting, and class-solidarity history.

One Clean Idea

Debs asks whether democracy can hear working people when wealth, courts, employers, and war pressure push the other way. He is the American labor doorway because he connects railroad work, union defeat, Socialist Party campaigns, public speech, anti-war prosecution, and class solidarity in one life.
