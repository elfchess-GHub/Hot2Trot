Why Trump Is Here

Trump belongs here because modern capitalism is not only factories or technology. It is real estate, branding, licensing, debt, media attention, inheritance, litigation, and political office.

He matters because his path makes the border between private business, public power, media spectacle, and political loyalty impossible to ignore.

One Clean Idea

Capital can become political power when property, brand, media attention, party politics, and state authority reinforce one another. Trump is not on the page as a cartoon villain; he is there because modern capitalism can turn ownership, debt, spectacle, and office into one public-force machine.
