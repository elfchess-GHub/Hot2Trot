Ayn Rand

1905-1982 | Topic: Objectivism / Laissez-Faire Capitalism | Era: Popular capitalist philosophy and anti-collectivism (1940-1982)

Role: Writer and philosopher who popularized Objectivism, rational self-interest, individualism, property rights, and laissez-faire capitalism.

Loading Wiki image

Why Rand Is Here

Rand belongs here because she made capitalism a moral story for millions of readers, not just an economic model.

She matters because Objectivism turns private property, individual achievement, self-interest, and anti-collectivism into a full political philosophy.

One Clean Idea

Rand's capitalism argument says individual rights and productive self-interest are moral foundations, not embarrassments to apologize for.
