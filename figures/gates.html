Bill Gates

1955- | Topic: Technology Capital / Philanthropy | Era: Software monopoly, global philanthropy, and platform wealth (1975-present)

Role: Microsoft cofounder, software capitalist, billionaire philanthropist, and central figure in debates over monopoly power and private public-good funding.

Loading Wiki image

Why Gates Is Here

Gates belongs here because modern capitalism is not only factories, railroads, and banks. It is also software platforms, intellectual property, network effects, antitrust law, and foundation power.

He matters because he shows both sides of concentrated wealth: Microsoft became a defining private technology empire, while Gates later used private philanthropy to shape global health and education debates.

One Clean Idea

A billionaire can fund public-good work and still raise democratic questions about monopoly power, private agenda-setting, and who gets to steer public life. Gates is the bridge from twentieth-century antitrust to twenty-first-century philanthropy and platform wealth.
