Industrial Revolution

1760s | Black road

Factories, wage labor, urban poverty, productivity, and the modern labor question intensify.

[source supports this]

What Happened?

The Industrial Revolution shifted production toward machines, factories, fossil energy, wage labor, urban growth, new transport, and large increases in output. It began unevenly, with Britain as an early center, and then spread into other regions and industries.

Why This Stop Matters

This stop matters because the modern labor question explodes here. Factories create wealth and productivity, but also long hours, unsafe work, child labor, crowded cities, and new class relations between owners and wage workers. Socialism becomes a mass argument in response to this industrial world.

Key Terms

Factory systemWage laborUrbanizationIndustrial capitalismProductivityWorking classLabor conditions

What To Be Careful About

Do not describe industrialization as only progress or only misery. It raised productivity and transformed life, while also creating severe labor, health, housing, and class problems.
