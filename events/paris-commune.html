Paris Commune

1871 | Red road

A short-lived revolutionary government becomes a major symbol for later socialist, communist, and anti-authoritarian debates.

[source lead checked]

What Happened?

The Paris Commune was a short-lived revolutionary government in Paris in 1871 after France's defeat in the Franco-Prussian War and conflict between Paris and the national government. It became famous for experiments in local self-government, radical democracy, worker politics, and its violent suppression.

Why This Stop Matters

This stop matters because later socialists, communists, anarchists, and anti-authoritarian radicals all argued over what the Commune proved. Was it a model of workers' democracy, a warning about state power, or both? It becomes a memory object for the revolutionary road.

Key Terms

What To Be Careful About

Do not turn the Commune into a perfect myth. It was brief, pressured by war and conflict, and remembered differently by different socialist traditions.
