Labor Movement and Trade Unions

1800s-1900s | Red road

Workers organize strikes, unions, parties, and bargaining power against long hours, unsafe work, low wages, and employer domination.

What Happened?

Workers organized unions, strikes, mutual aid, parties, and bargaining campaigns to respond to wages, hours, safety, discipline, child labor, unemployment, and employer power. Labor movements developed differently across countries, trades, industries, and legal systems.

Why This Stop Matters

This stop matters because socialism is not only books by theorists. It is also workers organizing power at the job, in the street, and in elections. Labor movements turn the abstract idea of class into real organizations and conflicts.

Key Terms

What To Be Careful About

Do not pretend all labor politics is socialist, and do not pretend labor is outside politics. Unions can be reformist, radical, conservative, democratic, exclusionary, solidaristic, or contradictory.
