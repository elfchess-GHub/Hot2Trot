John Locke

1632-1704 | Topic: Capitalism / Liberalism | Era: Early foundations (1600-1800)

Role: Philosopher of property, liberal rights, toleration, and government by consent.

Loading Wiki image

Why Locke Is Here

Locke belongs here because he helps establish the liberal argument for natural rights, private property, consent of government, toleration, and resistance to tyranny.

That matters because later capitalism uses this language to defend property and markets, while socialism later asks whether property rights protect everyone equally, or protect owners more than workers.

One Clean Idea

If people have natural rights, including property, then government exists to protect those rights, and loses legitimacy when it violates them.
