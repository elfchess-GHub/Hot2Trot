Laissez-Faire

Plain definition: Laissez-faire is a view that economic life should be left largely to private exchange, property, contract, and competition with minimal state interference.

In the Rand path, laissez-faire becomes a moral demand for individual rights, not only an efficiency claim.

Why It Matters for Rand

Laissez-faire matters because many capitalist arguments treat government intervention as the main danger to freedom.
