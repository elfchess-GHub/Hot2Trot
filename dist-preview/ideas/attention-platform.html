Attention Platform

Plain definition: An attention platform is a privately controlled media or social platform that shapes what people see, share, monetize, fight over, or believe is visible.

In the Musk path, Twitter/X turns ownership into direct influence over a public communication arena.

Why It Matters Here

Attention platforms matter because speech, politics, advertising, identity, and markets now move through privately owned infrastructure. Owning the platform is not the same as owning every opinion on it, but it can shape moderation, visibility, monetization, and public conflict.
