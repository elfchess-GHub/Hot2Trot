Vanguard Party

Plain definition: A vanguard party is a revolutionary organization that claims it can organize and lead the working class toward political power.

In the Lenin path, the party is not just a campaign machine. It is the central tool for discipline, theory, strategy, and state power.

Why It Matters Here

Vanguard party matters because it separates Lenin's revolutionary road from democratic reform roads and from broad public-service arguments.
